title: "Iterating Instead of Restarting"
order: 3
duration: "5 min"

Beginners tend to throw away a mediocre response and write a whole new prompt from scratch. Experienced users almost never do this — they iterate inside the same conversation.

## Why iteration beats restarting

The AI already has your full context from earlier in the conversation. A follow-up like "make it shorter and less formal" gets you closer, faster, than a brand-new prompt that has to re-explain everything.

## Useful iteration phrases

- "Make this more \[specific/concise/casual\] — keep everything else the same."
- "That's close, but drop the third point and expand the first."
- "Give me three variations of that last paragraph, more different from each other this time."
- "Explain why you chose that structure, then try again avoiding it."

## When to actually restart

Restart the conversation, not just the prompt, when you've gone deep down an unproductive path and the earlier context is now actively confusing the AI — for example, after several failed attempts at the same thing. A fresh conversation with a better first prompt often outperforms a long, tangled one.

## Exercise

Next time an AI response is "almost right," resist the urge to rewrite the whole prompt. Give one specific iteration instruction instead, and notice how much faster you converge on something usable.
